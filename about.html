Our Story

Built by a student. For a continent.

SRH was born out of a simple question: why are the people doing the most for South Africa's environment paid the least and protected the least?

JH

Joshua Haswell

Founder & Developer
ST10437907 · IIE Varsity College
ENTP6211w Entrepreneurship

Every morning on the way to campus I'd pass reclaimers pushing trolleys piled three metres high along the N1 — risking their lives for around R1,000 a month. Meanwhile, the country was burying 90% of its recyclable waste.

SRH started as a feasibility study for my entrepreneurship module. It grew into a full business model: AI-powered smart bins placed where people already gather — taxi ranks, schools, malls, markets — that pay users instantly in rewards they actually need.

No smartphone required. No ID required. No middlemen. Just deposit, scan, earn.

The mission is simple: turn every South African into a recycler — and turn every kilogram of waste into dignity, income and a cleaner future.
