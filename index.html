How It Works

Four steps. Real rewards.

Built for everyone — no smartphone, no ID, no friction.

1

Find a Hub

Locate an SRH bin at your nearest taxi rank, school, mall or market.

2

Deposit & Scan

Drop in PET, glass, paper, aluminium or mixed plastic. AI sorts it. Scan your QR card — no smartphone needed.

3

Earn Points

Every deposit grows your balance. The more you recycle, the higher your tier.

4

Redeem Rewards

Convert points to cash, airtime, data, grocery vouchers or transport credits.
