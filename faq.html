Frequently Asked

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about how SRH works for users, communities and partners.

Do I need a smartphone to use SRH?

No. SRH is designed for inclusivity. You get a free QR card at any hub, or you can use biometric (fingerprint) access. No phone, data or ID required.

What can I deposit?

PET bottles, glass bottles and jars, paper, newspaper, cardboard, aluminium cans and mixed plastic. Our AI conveyor system sorts everything automatically.

How fast do I get my rewards?

Instantly. The moment your items are sorted, points hit your account. You can redeem cash, airtime, data, transport credits or grocery vouchers right at the hub or via SMS.

How are points calculated?

Each material has a points value (PET = 2, Glass = 3, Aluminium = 4, Newspaper = 5, Mixed Plastic = 1). Higher tiers earn 25–50% bonus points on every deposit.

Where are the bins located?

SRH bins are placed in high-traffic public areas: taxi ranks, schools, shopping centres, university campuses, train stations and informal markets — places people pass through daily.

Are the bins safe from vandalism?

Yes. Storage is underground in tamper-proof containers. Above ground there's only the deposit slot, scanner and education display.

What happens to non-recyclables?

Anything that can't be recycled is converted to clean energy through our waste-to-energy partners — nothing is wasted.

How does SRH help waste-pickers?

We work with reclaimer co-operatives to formalise their work. Pickers can become hub stewards, sorters and trainers — earning a steady, dignified income off the highways.

Can businesses or schools host a hub?

Absolutely. Visit the Partners page or email partners@srh.co.za to discuss hosting an SRH bin at your site.

Is my data private?

Yes. Personal info is encrypted and never sold. We only license anonymised, aggregated recycling-behaviour insights — never individual data.
